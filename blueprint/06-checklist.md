# Checklist

Run through this before anything leaves `builds/`.

**Voice**
- [ ] Headline is 5 words or fewer.
- [ ] No exclamation marks, no "please", no puns, no speech words.
- [ ] Names spelled right: **Rufus Potié**, **Hudson Biggar**. President first.

**Look**
- [ ] Only blueprint colors. Paper + ink + at most one of forest/stone.
- [ ] Only the three blueprint fonts, three sizes max.
- [ ] At least 40% empty space.
- [ ] One focal point.
- [ ] `POTIÉ / BIGGAR` label present, in the series' corner.
- [ ] Square corners, no shadows, gradients, stickers or sparkles.

**Output**
- [ ] Correct size from `05-formats.md`.
- [ ] Passes the black-and-white test (print pieces).
- [ ] Text readable at arm's length (posters) or on a phone at a glance (social).
- [ ] Editable source linked next to the export.

**The gut check**
- [ ] Put it next to a competitor's poster. Does ours look calmer and more expensive? If not, remove something.
