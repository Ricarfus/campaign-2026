# Voice

## Rules
1. **Short.** A headline is 1–5 words. Body copy is a few short sentences at most.
2. **Plain.** Say it the way you'd say it to a friend in the hall. No speech words ("empower", "together we can", "make a difference").
3. **Calm.** No exclamation marks. No ALL CAPS shouting in sentences (all caps is fine for small labels and names, see type).
4. **Dry, not jokey.** Understatement over puns. One quiet line beats a clever one.
5. **Specific.** When we promise something, it's a real thing with a real detail. No vague promises.
6. **Never beg.** No "please vote", "vote 4 me", "every vote counts".

## Casing
- Names and labels: uppercase in the display or mono font (`POTIÉ / BIGGAR`).
- Headlines and body: sentence case.
- Keep the accent: **Potié**, never "Potie" (only drop it in URLs, handles and file names).

## How we write the ticket
- Full: `Rufus Potié for President. Hudson Biggar for Vice President.`
- Short / label: `POTIÉ / BIGGAR`
- Always President first.

## Tone check
| Instead of | Write something like |
| --- | --- |
| "Your voice, your future!" | "Noted." |
| "Vote for a brighter future!!" | "Potié / Biggar. That's it." |
| "We will make school more fun!" | Name one real, specific thing and stop. |
| "Please please vote for us" | Nothing. Just the names and the date. |

The right-hand column shows the tone, not approved slogans. Real lines get written once the platform is set.

## Emoji and hashtags
None on posters and flyers. On social, at most one plain hashtag if the school requires it. No emoji.
